Values

3T (Togetherness, Totality and Transcendental)

Mitra Group's values are based on appreciating its people, facing every demanding situation together in a productive working environment, placing greater importance for its people rather than the profit generated.

Simultaneously we also consider the importance of nurturing our people, to grow well both interpersonally, professionally and to reveal the true potential they possess, not only by getting the most out of the employees but also bringing out the best in the employees. This in turn will eventually result in the employees give their totality and dedication to the work performed. Mitra Group treats its people as an asset worth treasuring and at the same time developing them to possess a positive mental attitude—most importantly, our organization must transcend beyond the company's vision and lead into the right direction.

Vision

Our vision is to achieve success of our trading activities by expanding our market with products that satisfy our customer's needs. This will be accomplished by developing a distinguished brand synonymous with quality and a good reputation. Through this, we will be a reliable partner for our customers, suppliers and our people and this will enable us to share the success together.
